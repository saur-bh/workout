Progressive overload

Week Rule
1 Establish loads. Finish strength with ~2–3 reps in reserve (RIR).
2 Add 1–2 reps per set. If at top of range with perfect form, use smallest DB/KB increment.
3 Use slower eccentrics and pauses (mostly 3-1-1-0) to raise tension without heavier equipment.
4 Add ~2–5% load / next safe implement and work at ~1–2 RIR. No ugly reps, no grinding.

7-day menu

Food rules

  1. Half of lunch/dinner volume from vegetables: capsicum, carrot, radish, spinach, broccoli, cabbage, mushrooms and onions.
  2. Prefer lean chicken and low-fat Skyr/yogurt/paneer. Keep cheese/paneer portions controlled because saturated fat can climb quickly.
  3. Main carbs: rajma/lentils/chickpeas, besan, oats, kuttu, quinoa, brown rice, berries and whole fruit. Avoid juice and refined sweets as routine foods.
  4. Do not add a BCAA supplement. One normal whey serving is enough if daily protein is already on target.
  5. Whenever practical, walk 10–20 minutes after the largest meal. This is extra lifestyle movement, not part of the 20-minute workout.

How your report changed the plan

Genetics treated as modifiers, not diagnoses. Plan emphasizes repeated, low-risk recommendations in the report.

Blood sugar / insulinYour report flags impaired insulin secretion risk and poorer insulin sensitivity, and specifically says a low-glycaemic-load diet can benefit you. Hence legumes, vegetables, low-GL carbs, resistance training and aerobic work.
Meal timingThe melatonin/glucose section warns against late-night food. Your 11:00–18:00 window fits the "earlier calories" direction, provided it remains sustainable.
Saturated fat + BCAAReport repeatedly warns that high saturated fat + high BCAA intake may worsen insulin function. Hence lean protein, low-fat dairy and no BCAA supplement.
Strength potentialVEGFA section calls you a high responder to traditional resistance training. 3 of 5 training days are resistance-biased.
VO₂ maxAverage VO₂-max responder; report recommends HIIT 1–2×/week + steady aerobic work. Tuesday = main intervals; Thu/Fri add aerobic stimulus.
Soft tissue / boneCOL5A1 flags slightly higher soft-tissue injury risk; WNT16/VDR sections flag lower BMD tendency. Plan uses progressive loading, controlled eccentrics and weight-bearing work rather than daily maximal impact.
SleepCLOCK section links fragmented/short sleep with overeating and weight-gain risk. Min target 7 h; preferred 7–9 h.
CaffeineIntermediate caffeine metaboliser with CV-risk flags at higher intake. Staying caffeine-free is a sensible fit for your stated preference.
Safety gate: if your blood pressure is currently uncontrolled, or you get chest pain, faintness, unusual breathlessness or palpitations, get medical clearance before the hard interval sessions. Exhale through the hard part of strength reps and avoid prolonged breath-holding.
